The research behind the chip.

Forty-plus years of papers, from the first systolic arrays to optically switched TPU pods. Every entry says what it contributed and where you can see that idea running in this repo. Filter by topic, search, or click a dot on the timeline.

Where to start

A four-paper reading path

Why systolic?

Kung, 1982. Eight pages that explain the whole idea: pump data through a grid of simple cells so every number is used many times.

The first TPU

Jouppi et al., 2017. Read it next to the 3-D tour: the block diagram is the same one TinyTPU copies.

Energy is the budget

Sze et al., 2017 (survey) and Horowitz, 2014. Why dataflow and data movement decide everything. Then run the race.

Scaling out

TPU v4, 2023. Pods of 4,096 chips wired by optical switches. The same "neighbors pass data" idea, at building scale.
