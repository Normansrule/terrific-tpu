Systolic array playground

A cycle-by-cycle model of the 4 x 4 Matrix Multiply Unit in rtl/systolic_array.v. Weights stay put in the grid, input vectors slide in from the left on a staircase, and partial sums trickle down until finished answers drop out of the bottom. Edit any number, then step the clock.

Weights W (stay in the grid)

W[row][col] sits inside PE(row, col).

Input vectors X (slide in from the left)

Each row of X is one vector. Its element k enters array row k.

Part of the terrific-tpu repository. Same timing as the Verilog: vector v is inside PE(row k, column n) during cycle v + k + n, and finished results need 2N − 1 = 7 cycles to come out lined up.
