A virtual Basys 3.

This is the same design you load onto a real Digilent Basys 3: the same four baked-in demos, the same switches, buttons, LEDs and display, running on the cycle-exact chip models. Click the switches and buttons. What you see here is what the board shows: the cycle counts match the Verilog. When you're ready for real hardware, follow lesson 22.

What's happening

Pick a demo with switches 1–0, then press the centre button.
Switches 1–0 pick the demo: 00 neural net, 01 Fourier, 10 image filter, 11 Grover.
Press C (centre): load, run, self-check. The display shows PASS.
Press R for the cycle count, again for pc + operation, again for a result value.
Flip switch 15 on, press C, then press U to tick the clock once. Watch LEDs 0–4.
