Numbers and the roofline

Two questions decide whether a TPU is fast for a job: how few bits can the numbers use, and does the chip spend its time computing or waiting for memory?

How many bits do we need?

A trained layer's weights (blue) rounded to fewer bits (orange). Fewer bits means smaller multipliers and less memory traffic, but more rounding error.
